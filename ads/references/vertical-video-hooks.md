# Vertical Video Hooks — Viral Hook Library

<!-- Sources: OpusClip, Teleprompter, Vexub, SocialKit, Retensis, Darkroom (2026 research) -->

## Purpose

The hook decides ~80% of a vertical video's fate. TikTok's algorithm makes its
first quality judgment in ~1.5 seconds; viewers decide to stay or swipe in
1.5-3 seconds. YouTube Shorts: if a viewer survives the first 2 seconds,
probability of full completion rises ~60%.

## Hook Benchmarks

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| 3-second hold rate | **70-80%+** | Videos holding 70-85% at 3s get ~2.2x more views |
| First-2s swipe-away (Shorts) | **<40%** | "Viewed vs Swiped" >50% = strong; <30% kills reach |
| Hold rate (3s → 75% mark) | 15-25% Meta, 10-18% TikTok | Healthy cold traffic |
| Spoken hook length | **10-14 words max** | Must land inside 3 seconds |
| Text hook length | 3-8 words | First frame, bold sans, upper third |

## The Four Hook Layers (stack 2-3 simultaneously)

| Layer | Execution |
|-------|-----------|
| **Visual** | Show finished result/transformation first; movement toward camera; unusual object in frame 1 |
| **Verbal** | 10-14 words, start mid-sentence/mid-action — never "hey guys", never an intro |
| **Text** | On-screen headline in first frame, 3-8 words, upper-third, inside safe zone |
| **Pattern interrupt** | Break feed expectations: abrupt sound, odd framing, unexpected motion, silence |

## Hook Formulas (ranked by 2026 performance)

1. **Outcome Showcase** — show the end result in the first 2s, then explain how.
   Highest-performing type overall (OpusClip data).
2. **Transformation Numbers** — "I went from [bad number] to [good number] in
   [timeframe] using [thing]". Highest-converting archetype across platforms.
3. **Mistake Warning (negative)** — "Stop doing X if you want Y". Negative
   framings produce **1.3-1.8x higher hook rate** than positive equivalents.
4. **Contrarian Claim** — "Most people get this wrong about X…"
5. **List Tease** — "3 things nobody tells you about X…" (put the best item
   LAST — open loop)
6. **Identity Call** — "If you're a [specific person] who [specific problem]…"
   Top retention on Reels; specificity beats reach for small accounts.
7. **POV Framing** — "POV: you just found the hack that saves you 2 hours…"
8. **Confessional** — "I wish I knew this sooner…"

## Hook Templates (fill-in)

```
- "Nobody talks about the [X] that [outcome]"
- "This [thing] is why your [area] isn't [desired result]"
- "I tested [X] for [timeframe] — here's what actually happened"
- "The [industry] doesn't want you to know this about [topic]"
- "You're [common action] wrong. Here's the fix:"
- "[Impressive number/result] — and it took me [surprisingly small effort]"
- "Watch what happens when I [unexpected action]"
- "Before you [common action], watch this"
```

## Platform Nuances

- **TikTok**: sound-on platform — verbal hook + trending-sound pattern
  interrupt carry the most weight. Cold start pool judges the hook regardless
  of follower count.
- **Reels**: larger share watch muted in-feed — visual + text hooks carry more
  relative weight than verbal. First frame doubles as the grid cover.
- **Shorts**: swipe decision is fastest (2s). Front-load motion; avoid any
  static first frame.

## Re-Hook (seconds 3-8)

The hook buys 3 seconds; the re-hook buys the video:
- Raise stakes or add context in ONE sentence ("and it cost me nothing")
- Schedule the first big pattern interrupt **before second 5-6** — retention
  typically collapses around second 6 when rhythm becomes predictable
- Never spend the re-hook window on intros, logos, or channel branding

## Writing Process (Script mode)

1. Write the video body first; write the hook LAST
2. Generate **5+ hook variants** across at least 3 formulas above
3. Pick 3 to test; keep body identical across variants (single-variable test)
4. Verify each variant works muted (text layer) AND blind (verbal layer)
5. First frame check: would this frame stop a scroll as a static image?

## Hook Audit Checklist

| # | Check | FAIL signal |
|---|-------|-------------|
| H1 | Something moves or changes within 1s | Static first frame |
| H2 | Verbal hook complete by 3s | Intro, greeting, or logo first |
| H3 | Text hook on frame 1, inside safe zone | No text layer, or covered by UI |
| H4 | Hook creates an open question | Hook summarizes (nothing left to learn) |
| H5 | Hook promise is paid off in the video | Bait — kills follows and account trust |
| H6 | 3s hold rate ≥70% | Cliff drop at 0-3s in retention curve |
